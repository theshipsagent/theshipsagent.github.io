Our mission

The freedom to roam Panama

To hand travellers the freedom, beauty and adventure of Panama — on their own time and their own road. No fixed itinerary, no crowds, no rush: just a van that's ready and a country that rewards getting lost.

The idea

Simple on purpose

OnlyVans Panamá is three fully-built camper vans and a flat daily price. You pick one, we check it's free, and you drive off into the part of Panama that doesn't fit on a tour brochure — Caribbean cayes, jungle rivers, empty point breaks.

Each van sleeps, cooks, charges and showers off-grid, so where you wake up is entirely up to you. Stay a night near the surf, a week up the coast — there's no itinerary to keep.

More than a van

A base camp for Bocas

Want a roof over your head for part of the trip too? We can point you to places to stay, eat and play across Bocas del Toro — from the water to the jungle. The van is the start of the trip, not the whole of it.

How it started

From long flights to slow roads

After a career in shipping — busy cities, quick turnarounds, long flights — Claas fell for the freedom, beauty and lifestyle of Panama. Based in the unique archipelago of Bocas del Toro, OnlyVans Panamá turns that into something you can borrow: a camper, a coastline, and the time to follow it.
